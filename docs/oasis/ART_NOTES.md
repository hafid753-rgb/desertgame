# Oasis (Phase 3 lab) — art notes vs Garbage Country & peers

## Direction (owner, 2026-11-24)
Pixel art is a stylistic choice of the assets: **full resolution, full
polycount**. The Phase 1 low-res render layer is now off by default
(`Main.pixel_render_enabled = false`); it remains available as an option.

## What we take from Garbage Country / Noio
Source: Noio's Pixel-Unwrapper README (github.com/noio/Pixel-Unwrapper,
5665e5c): pixel-art 3D "looks best when pixels align with edges on models"
and "pixels are roughly the same size across the model".
- One texel density everywhere: 32 px/m (sand, water, earth, trunks via
  pixel-aligned UVs, blades/leaves sized from their sprite pixels).
- Simple, readable 3D forms carrying hand-made pixel textures; nearest
  sampling; no texture smoothing, no gloss, no heavy outlines.
- Vegetation as many small flat pixel cards in 3D (Cloud Gardens / Garbage
  Country foliage), each moving on its own.
Not available here: I could not view Garbage Country footage from this
workspace (no video/image access), so comparisons are against Noio's
documented principles, press descriptions and the brief, not frame-matched.

### Press / store descriptions (round 2, 2026-11-25)
- PC Gamer (PC Gaming Show 2025 coverage) sums the look up as "Wobbly
  PlayStation polygons. Dithered dust." -> dust and transparency are
  ordered dither, never soft alpha.
- Store copy: sun-bleached concrete, "the wind whistles as dust blows over a
  forgotten highway" -> wind is a visible actor: sand moving along the
  ground, not only plants swaying.
- 80.lv: Noio paints texels onto simple 3D models in Blender with his
  PixPaint plug-in; a separate 3D pixel-art artist names perfect UV
  unwrapping and texel density as what matters most.

### Noio in his own words (80.lv interview "Kingdom developer on creating pixel environments")
- Two rules: pixels align with geometry; pixels are the same size across
  the model. Hafid: met (32 px/m everywhere, prints resampled to the grid).
- Textures hand-painted (Photoshop, later in Blender); PixPaint only does
  the UV unwrapping. Hafid: sprites/textures are generated by editable
  Python sources (tools/pixel/*); hand-painting passes are still open.
- Lighting and rendering stripped back "closer to the pixel"; stock Unity
  apart from custom shaders. Hafid: same approach (palette light ramp,
  no gloss/specular, no outlines on the environment).
- Trees: branches drawn as curves, bevelled, cleaned up, textured; scattered
  with terrain tools. Hafid: acacia/palm branches are spline tubes with
  pixel-aligned UVs, placed procedurally.
- Water reflections: a second camera renders the scene from below.
  Hafid (round 3): `WaterReflection` — a mirrored camera renders only the
  REFLECT layer (palms, acacias, player) into a full-resolution SubViewport
  with transparent background; pixel_water samples it at (1-u, v), wobbles
  it with the ripple bands, tints it with the water and dithers it onto the
  world texel grid. The sky stays the stylised band. Skipped when the pond
  is off-screen or >60 m away. `Oasis.reflections` turns it off.
  Measured, arrival view, software renderer: 444k -> 525k primitives,
  179 -> 233 draws, ~230 -> ~260 ms (about +13-18 %). Needs device check.

### Applied in round 2
| Garbage Country trait | Hafid implementation |
|---|---|
| Dithered dust | `dust_dither.gdshader` (footstep/landing puffs, water spray), `wind_dust.gdshader` drift sheet + grains — all transparency is a 4x4 ordered dither on world texels (`bayer4()` in pixel_common) |
| Wind as a visible actor | Blown-sand streaks travel downwind and thicken under each gust front of the shared `Wind` field; grains hop in gusts; leaves shed from acacias drift, settle, slide in gusts |
| Constant texel density | Footprints are resampled onto the sand's own 32 px/m grid at any heading (`footprint.gdshader`), so prints never show rotated pixels; dust discs quantised to world texels |
| Traces of passage | Boot prints every 0.5 m, alternating feet, eroded by wind over ~40 s (faster in gusts) |
| Readable forms at a distance | Acacia canopy rebuilt from feathery bipinnate sprays (6x6 px pinnae every 3 texels along twigs), two tiers: sunlit top, darker underside |

Also: wading — splash of pixel droplets on entry, expanding crest/trough
ripple rings (8 slots, dithered as they age), lapping foam ring at the legs.

## Peers used as reference
- Journey: sand diffuse contrast, ripples, glitter (Zucconi series).
- Tunic: composition — clear landmark (palm grove + pond) framed by dunes,
  readable from the gameplay camera.
- Dylearn 3D pixel-art grass (MIT): character displacement idea (ours uses
  the player position global; theirs a 64-entry array).

## What was built
- `Wind` autoload + `wind.gdshaderinc` global uniforms: base breeze, gust
  fronts travelling downwind, turbulence, per-material response, sheltering
  hook, whole-plant sway; CPU `Wind.wind_at()` mirrors the shader.
- `veg_card.gdshader`: individual subdivided cards (blades 2x8 quads,
  leaflets 1x4, leaves 1x2), root fixed, h^2 bend, tip flutter, elastic by
  construction (stateless field), player parting, atlas per instance,
  4x4 ordered-dither camera fade, palette light ramp.
- `Vegetation`: date palm (pixel-UV trunk, 13 arching fronds, individual
  leaflets, dry fronds), acacia (forked branches, umbrella canopy of
  individual leaflets), thorny shrubs, flowers, MultiMesh chunks (6 m) with
  visibility ranges.
- `Oasis`: lobed pond with pixel water (dithered depth bands, wind ripples,
  foam lip, single-texel glints), damp shore, grass density from water
  distance + noise clumps (~7.7k blades), 4 palms, 2 acacias, 5 shrubs,
  9 rocks. Courtyard palms replaced by the new palms.
- Sprites: `tools/pixel/gen_vegetation.py` (editable source).

## Measured (software Vulkan under xvfb, full resolution, NOT device data)
Screenshot tour incl. courtyard: 12 fps mean, 79 ms mean frame,
158 draw calls, 144k primitives. Device profiling still required; the grass
chunk visibility range (55 m) and blade density are the first knobs.

## Known gaps
- Footprints are small at the default camera distance (true boot scale);
  they read best when the camera looks back along the trail.
- Prints also appear on the packed-earth courtyard floor (no surface type
  lookup yet).
- Grass does not flatten permanently where the player walked.
- Palm fronds do not shed (date palms rarely drop pinnae; skipped on purpose).
